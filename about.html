About the studio

Small stitches, studied closely.

Ribbing is an independent website about ribbed socks and the knits behind them.

We write about how socks are made, which fibres do what and how to care for a pair so it lasts. Our studio tools let you see how rib ratios and fibre blends behave.

We do not sell socks, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
